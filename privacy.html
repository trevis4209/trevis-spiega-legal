Privacy Policy

Trevis Spiega Uploader · Effective date: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

Trevis Spiega Uploader ("the App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Trevis Spiega to manage the channel's own videos. The data controller is Trevis Spiega, Italy. Contact: trevisspiega@gmail.com.

2. What data the App uses

  • Google account authorization (scopes youtube and youtube.force-ssl): an access token and a refresh token issued by Google for the channel owner's account.
  • The channel's own content: video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists, subtitles and translations that the channel owner uploads or edits.

The App does not access other users' accounts, comments, subscribers' personal data or viewing history.

3. Why

Only to upload and manage the channel owner's own videos on YouTube, at the owner's request. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and it is not sold or shared.

4. Storage and retention

Tokens are stored encrypted by the credential service used to call the YouTube API and are deleted when access is revoked. Video files stay on the owner's computer; the App does not keep copies after the upload.

5. Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own terms apply to uploaded content: YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Your rights and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to the contact above; you may also complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
